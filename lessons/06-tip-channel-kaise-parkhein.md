# 6. Tip channel kaise parkhein

Telegram aur WhatsApp par hazaron "tips" channel hain. Paisa dene ya unki baat maanne se pehle ye check kijiye.

## ✅ Check list

1. **SEBI registration number maangiye.** Research Analyst ka number `INH...` se aur Investment Adviser ka `INA...` se shuru hota hai. Use [SEBI ki website](https://www.sebi.gov.in) par verify kijiye. India mein paise lekar tips dena bina registration ke allowed nahi hai.
2. **Poora track record maangiye**, loss wali trades bhi. Sirf screenshots kaafi nahi hain.
3. **Cost ke baad ka result poochiye** ([Lesson 4](04-cost.md)).

## 🚩 Red flags

- "Guaranteed return", "sure shot", "jackpot", "100% accuracy"
- Free channel se paid VIP group mein le jaane ki jaldi
- Phone number, broker ID, password, OTP ya "account handling" maangna
- Sirf profit wale screenshots, loss kabhi nahi
- "Aaj hi join karo, sirf 10 seat bachi hain" jaisa dabav

## Hamara ek asli example

Humne ek "AI trading" channel ki ek mahine ki saari calls nikali. Unke **khud ke posted results** ke hisaab se 72 trades mein average **-0.08R** prati trade aaya, yani cost ke baad ghata. Channel par "Quality 95/100" aur "AI powered" likha tha, par numbers kuch aur keh rahe the.

**Seekh:** label nahi, data dekhiye.
