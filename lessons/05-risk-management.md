# 5. Risk management

Acha model bhi galat risk ke saath account doob sakta hai.

## Basic niyam

- **Ek trade mein capital ka 0.5-1% se zyada risk nahi.** ₹1 lakh par ek trade mein ₹500-1,000 tak ka loss.
- **Stop loss pehle tay karo**, trade lene se pehle. Trade lene ke baad use door mat khiskao.
- **Din ka max loss tay karo.** Wahan pahunch gaye to us din trading band.
- **Ek hi sector ya group mein saara paisa nahi.** Hamare paper portfolios mein ek industry mein max 5 aur ek business group mein max 2 stocks rakhte hain.
- **Leverage aur option buying mein loss bahut tez hota hai.** Premium 0 tak ja sakta hai.

## Position size ka simple formula

```
Quantity = (Capital × Risk%) ÷ (Entry − Stop loss)
```

**Example:** ₹1,00,000 capital, 1% risk = ₹1,000. Entry ₹500, stop ₹490, yani ₹10 ka risk prati share.
Quantity = 1,000 ÷ 10 = **100 shares**.

## Options mein dhyan

Option premium index se alag chalta hai. Hamare Nifty agent mein shuru mein maan liya tha ki premium index ka aadha chalega, par asal mein 0.5x se 0.9x chala. Isse loss plan se 1.1-1.9 guna ho gaye. Iske baad index stop ke saath premium par bhi stop loss lagaya.
