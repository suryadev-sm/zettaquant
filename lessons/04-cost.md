# 4. Cost sab kuch hai

Har trade ka kharcha hota hai:

- Brokerage
- STT (Securities Transaction Tax)
- Exchange aur SEBI charges, GST, stamp duty
- **Spread:** buy aur sell price ka farq
- **Slippage:** jis price par chahte the aur jis par mila, uska farq

Intraday aur scalping mein ye sab milakar har round trip par 0.05% se 0.2% tak ho sakta hai.

## Seedha niyam

> Agar ek trade ki **gross kamai cost se kam** hai, to strategy bekaar hai, chahe accuracy kitni bhi achi dikhe.

## Hamare asli examples

| Model | Kamai prati trade | Cost | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scalp (Nifty 1-min) | ~4 bps | ~7 bps | ❌ Band |
| Max (stocks intraday) | 14.5 bps | 16 bps | ❌ Band |
| Swing (2-10 din) | 44 bps | 30 bps | Cost ke baad positive, par benchmark se peeche |

*(1 bps = 0.01%)*

Isliye jab koi channel "90% accuracy" dikhaye, to poochiye: **cost ke baad har trade mein kitna bacha?**
